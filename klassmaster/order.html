Buy a Zelix KlassMaster™ License

Zelix KlassMaster™ is licensed on either a machine or a site basis. One standard license allows you to run Zelix KlassMaster™ on one machine. So you need to purchase a license for each machine on which Zelix KlassMaster™ will be run. On the other hand, a site license allows you to use Zelix KlassMaster™ on any number of machines at one physical location. We at Zelix are committed to ensuring that Zelix KlassMaster™ is accessible to small developers. This is reflected in our pricing policy.

Note that all prices do not include any VAT or Sales Tax that may be payable.

Also note that if you need to purchase more than one license then it is cheaper to purchase them in one go as a multi-license or site license rather than as a number of separate licenses.

Standard Price: USD 585.
Applies if your organization (i.e. your company) consists of three or more people or has employed or contracted three or more people at any one time in the twelve months prior to the order date.
Small Developer Price: USD 290.
Applies if your organization (i.e. your company) consists of no more than two people and has employed or contracted no more than two people at any one time in the twelve months prior to the order date.

Please note that, if you purchase a license, you will not be shipped any tangible goods such as a CD-ROM or a printed manual. Rather, a copy of the non-evaluation software will be e-mailed to an address nominated by you on the order form. The software will be customized to show licensee details and the number of licenses purchased.

Before placing an order, please read the Zelix KlassMaster™ license conditions carefully. Please see below for the various methods of payment.
