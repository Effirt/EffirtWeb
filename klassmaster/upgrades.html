Overview

You can either
  1. upgrade your Zelix KlassMaster™ version (i.e. upgrade to the latest version) or
  2. upgrade Zelix KlassMaster™ your license type (e.g. increase the number of machines covered by your license).

Version Upgrade Policy

If you license a copy of Zelix KlassMaster
  • you are entitled to a free upgrade to any subsequent minor release. So, if you license Zelix KlassMaster™ 27.0.0 then you are entitled to a free upgrade to versions 27.0.1, 27.0.2 and so on.
  • you are entitled to a free upgrade to any major release that is released within three months of the purchase date.
We notify subscribers to the Zelix KlassMaster™ Announcements mailing list when major new versions are released but we do not automatically e-mail upgrades. If you are entitled to a free or reduced cost upgrade please send an e-mail to quoting your Zelix KlassMaster™ license number1. Your upgrade will then be e-mailed to you.

Otherwise you can purchase a Zelix KlassMaster™ upgrade using a secure online order form via one of the following links. Again, you will be asked to quote your existing Zelix KlassMaster™ license number1.

Note that all prices do not include any VAT or Sales Tax that may be payable.

1 Your Zelix KlassMaster™ license number appears on the initial "splash" screen when you start Zelix KlassMaster™ in GUI mode and it will be written to standard output (i.e. the command line) when you run a ZKM Script. It will also be written to the Zelix KlassMaster™ log file which is named "ZKM_log.txt" by default.
